About ArtisanAI

A Smart India Hackathon solution for artisan empowerment through AI.

❗ The Problem

Millions of marginalized artisans create beautiful handicrafts but lack access to digital markets, photography, marketing language and fair pricing knowledge — leaving them dependent on middlemen who take most of the profit.

💡 Our Solution

ArtisanAI is a low-literacy-friendly platform where artisans photograph a product and AI handles the rest: image enhancement, bilingual catalog writing, fair price suggestions and a direct-to-buyer marketplace.

How AI powers each step

🖼️

Computer Vision

Background removal, lighting correction and scene generation for professional product shots.

🗣️

Generative Language Models

Bilingual titles, descriptions and SEO tags generated from a few spoken or typed details.

📊

Market Data Modeling

Pricing suggestions that account for material cost, labour time and regional market rates.
